Read the ranges as compatibility, not confidence. The bounds hold the 240-bin support, inferred carry and selected quote family fixed, while allowing any compatible density shape. Very small chosen tail estimates can have much larger feasible upper bounds. The common-contract control changes the quote sample and re-estimates carry; its ranges are not calculated here. All observations were inspected during design. One late-snapshot validation fold failed the numerical acceptance rule, and successful held-out checks still contain spread misses. In-sample compatibility does not establish density accuracy or predictive performance.
