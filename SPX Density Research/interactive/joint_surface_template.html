Validation, numerical tolerances and sources

As Gatheral and Jacquier explain in Arbitrage-free SVI volatility surfaces [12, §2], consistency across maturities is a separate condition from valid individual slices. The common-bin representation lets this project check quadratic call-price gaps between bin boundaries. As Stellato and co-authors describe in their OSQP paper [13], convex quadratic programmes can be solved to specified residual tolerances.

The synthetic quote inputs, 120 bins, support [0.3F, 2.2F], smoothing penalty and forward assumptions are unchanged. Small signed solver residuals are retained; masses are not clipped or renormalised. Passing this check is not an empirical market result, an exact symbolic proof, or a guarantee of accurate unobserved tails.

Bahra's Bank of England paper [1] remains the foundation for the risk-neutral interpretation; these densities are not physical-probability forecasts. Historical-date animation remains a later stage dependent on verified snapshots.

[1] Bahra, Bank of England Working Paper 66.
[12] Gatheral and Jacquier, Arbitrage-free SVI volatility surfaces.
