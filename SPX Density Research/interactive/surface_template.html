Validation, assumptions and research context

Each fit enforces nonnegative bin probabilities, unit total probability and a mean equal to the supplied forward. These are constraints, not independent evidence that the estimated shape is correct. Withheld-strike errors are synthetic interpolation checks.

As Gatheral and Jacquier explain in Arbitrage-free SVI volatility surfaces [12, §2], cross-maturity consistency is a separate requirement from the validity of individual slices. This project tests normalised call prices at fixed forward moneyness; it does not implement their SVI estimator. As Bahra explains in his Bank of England working paper [1], these risk-neutral distributions should not be interpreted as real-world forecasts.
