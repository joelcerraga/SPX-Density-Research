Reading the evidence. The primary selection chose λ = 10⁻⁷ from its declared three-value grid. Weaker coefficients were examined after seeing that boundary result and are labelled post-hoc. All fits meet the numerical probability and calendar criteria, but neither those checks nor a low price residual establishes density accuracy. There is no known market density for comparison. The fit targets call midpoints; call–put parity supplies put values, and some prices remain outside their spreads. Very small extrapolated tail probabilities are particularly sensitive to smoothing.
